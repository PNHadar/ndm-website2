Team 02 • Cash, Paulk, Zelmann & Hadar Labs

Cognition & Sleep

Investigating how cortical slow oscillations, thalamic spindles, and hippocampal sharp-wave ripples coordinate human single-neuron ensembles during language, working memory, and sleep.

← All Research Teams Meet Team Researchers →
Team 02 • Cash, Paulk, Zelmann & Hadar Labs

Core Scientific Questions & Translational Goals

  • How do human cortical columns encode semantic concepts, speech production, and executive decision-making at single-neuron resolution?
  • How do thalamocortical oscillations orchestrate offline memory consolidation during NREM sleep?
  • How does epileptic or peritumoral activity disrupt sleep architecture and cognitive recovery?
