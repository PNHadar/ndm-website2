The 5 Collaborative Research Teams

Alternating editorial deep-dives into our five core research programs.

TEAM 01
Cash, Paulk, Zelmann & Salami Labs

Epilepsy & Neuromodulation

Multi-scale intracranial electrophysiology, seizure onset/termination network mapping, and state-dependent direct electrical brain stimulation (SPES/CCEP & CLoSES).

Open Team Portal →
TEAM 02
Cash, Paulk, Zelmann & Hadar Labs

Cognition & Sleep

Investigating how cortical slow oscillations, thalamic spindles, and hippocampal ripples coordinate single-neuron ensembles during human language, memory, and sleep.

Open Team Portal →
TEAM 03
Cash, Paulk, Zelmann & Hadar Labs

Neurotechnology

High-density intraoperative Neuropixels, PEDOT:PSS organic micro-ECoG grids, CLoSES closed-loop neuromodulation hardware, and BrainGate intracortical interfaces.

Open Team Portal →
TEAM 04
Paulk & Hadar Labs

Cancer Neuroscience

Unraveling neuron-glioma interactions, peritumoral hyperexcitability, and high-resolution awake intraoperative brain mapping to protect cognitive function during tumor resection.

Open Team Portal →
TEAM 05
Cash & Hadar Labs

AI in Neurology and Neuroscience of AI

Intracranial foundation models, clinical neuro-AI biomarkers, and neuroscience-inspired mechanistic interpretability & representation engineering (virtual reading and stimulation of deep neural networks).

Open Team Portal →
