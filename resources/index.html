Pipelines, Progress & Open Neurotechnology

Following the open-science ethos of our laboratories, we share our real-time closed-loop stimulation platforms, spike-sorting pipelines, intracranial atlases, and peer-reviewed publications with the global neuroscience community.

Open-Source Software, Hardware & Clinical Platforms

GitHub • Real-Time Neuromodulation

CLoSES (Closed-Loop Stimulation System)

Open-source C++/MATLAB/Python platform for real-time intracranial feature extraction, biomarker decoding, and state-dependent electrical stimulation in the human EMU and operating room.

Explore CLoSES on GitHub ↗
DANDI & Open Data • Single-Neuron Atlas

Human Cortical Neuropixels & PEDOT:PSS Datasets

Curated intraoperative laminar single-unit and local field potential recordings across human prefrontal, temporal, and motor cortex accompanied by drift-correction pipelines.

Access Datasets & Code ↗
Clinical AI & Consortia • CDAC & BrainGate

CDAC & BrainGate Neural Interface Ecosystem

Large-scale intracranial/scalp neurophysiology foundation models (Clinical Data Animation Center) and intracortical brain-computer interface trials restoring communication and mobility.

Visit BrainGate.org ↗
